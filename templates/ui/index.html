How to use this tool
1. Add — click "+ Add" to record a new invoice or reimbursement claim.
2. Generate — click Generate to produce a PDF from that row. Re-gen overwrites it.
3. Send — click View to open the PDF in a new tab. Download or print it to send to your client.
4. Track status — update the status on each row manually as things progress. draft just created, not yet sent   sent sent to client, awaiting payment   paid payment received — record the date and reference in Edit
5. Notes — use the Notes field (in Edit) to record anything relevant: partial payment details, client contact, disputes, or follow-up dates.

Type badges: service invoice for work done   reimb. reimbursement claim — attach a receipt before generating
